About Us

Meet the Mind Behind Maven Masters

A small team that reads every application properly. We build relationships and deliver results.

Mr. Deepesh Gupta

Founder & Director, Maven Masters

Mr. Deepesh Gupta is the Founder and Director of Maven Masters, an organization dedicated to providing academic support, research guidance, and career mentoring to students, researchers, and professionals. With nearly two decades of experience working with both international and national institutions, he has established himself as a leader in educational empowerment.

Mr. Gupta holds an advanced research degree from the University of Queensland, Australia, where he was awarded the prestigious University of Queensland International Scholarship. His impressive research portfolio includes publications in high-impact journals and over 3,000 citations, demonstrating his significant contributions to academia.

Throughout his career, he has collaborated with universities, colleges, and institutions to conduct workshops on critical topics including academic writing, research methodology, publication ethics, skill development, and career planning. He has also served as Assistant Professor at various academic institutions, where he mentored students across diverse subjects.

His teaching philosophy emphasizes bridging theory and practice, making complex academic concepts accessible and application-oriented through practical insights combined with academic rigor.

Under Mr. Gupta's leadership, Maven Masters has evolved into a trusted platform known for its ethical approach and commitment to student success. He remains passionate about mentoring young minds and continues to actively contribute to academic and professional development initiatives across various institutions.

Where can I get detailed information about your services?

We have dedicated pages for Study Abroad, Academic Writing, and Career Counselling. Each page covers the process in detail, along with pricing and service-specific FAQs. You can also book a free call to discuss your specific needs.

No. We provide research guidance, structuring, editing, formatting, statistical support and similarity checks. The intellectual work stays with the scholar. We do not ghost-write work submitted for a degree.
